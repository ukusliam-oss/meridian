## Q1. For each candidate topic, which authoritative open datasets exist, and which can be vendored?

### Takeaway
Most topics can be covered by public-domain, CC0 or CC BY data: USGS, NOAA, NASA, GEBCO, Natural Earth, SCAR, Allen Coral Atlas, Hansen/UMD, Global Mangrove Watch, GHSL, WorldPop, World Bank, UN WPP and UNDP. Three need care: Smithsonian GVP volcanoes (Smithsonian Terms of Use, non-commercial and educational only, not a public licence), HydroSHEDS (its own licence agreement, which limits stand-alone redistribution) and Berkeley Earth (CC BY-NC). One should not be used: the UNEP-WCMC warm-water coral reef layer, whose licence forbids any redistribution, including through web downloads, web services and interactive maps.

## Q2. Licence, attribution and redistribution terms, and compatibility with a site that already ships CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 data

### Takeaway
Under CC rules, public-domain, CC0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY 3.0 IGO and ODC-By data can be redistributed raw or derived in a public GitHub repo with attribution, and can even be merged into a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 adaptation. CC BY-NC data (Berkeley Earth) is also usable on a non-commercial site. Three kinds of terms do not fit: custom terms that are not public licences (Smithsonian, HydroSHEDS, UNEP-WCMC), ODbL databases (OSM, the OSM-derived parts of WorldPop) and CC BY-SA. They cannot be relicensed into a CC BY-NC-SA file and must ship as separate works under their own terms, or not at all. Two terms changed recently: the Copernicus CDS licence (CC BY from 2 July 2025) and the NSIDC Sea Ice Index (Version 4, Basic service).

### Cited Findings
**Creative Commons combination rules**
- CC's compatible-licenses page says contributions to adaptations of BY-SA or BY-NC-SA works must use a licence on CC's approved list. — [CC compatible licenses](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/licensing-considerations/compatible-licenses)
- Secondary summaries of CC guidance: if a combination is not an adaptation (a collection), any CC content can be combined with proper attribution and compliance with NC terms. An adaptation of a BY work may be licensed BY or a more restrictive licence such as BY-NC. A BY-NC-SA work combined with a BY work can yield a BY-NC-SA adaptation compatible with both. — [Meadville Lombard library guide](https://library.meadville.edu/creative_commons/using); [Western Sydney CC compatibility](https://westernsydney.pressbooks.pub/cclicensingattribution/chapter/understanding-cc-licence-compatibility/); [UNC Wilmington guide](https://libguides.uncw.edu/creativecommons/reuse)
- BY-NC-SA and BY-SA cannot be combined into one adaptation, because one prohibits commercial use and the other permits it. — [Wikimedia Meta: Open Content guide](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Open_Content_-_A_Practical_Guide_to_Using_Creative_Commons_Licences/The_Creative_Commons_licencing_scheme)
- ND-licensed works cannot be used in publicly shared adaptations, and ShareAlike works require a compatible licence for the adaptation. — [University of Guelph guide](https://books.lib.uoguelph.ca/copyrightcc/?p=46)

## Q3. Practical facts: temporal coverage, resolution, raw size, web size, formats and build tools

### Takeaway
Time series (CO2, GISTEMP, sea ice, WPP, WDI, HDI) are tiny: kilobytes as JSON. Point and line layers (earthquakes, volcanoes, plates, storm tracks, rivers, reefs) need filtering and quantising to reach a few hundred KB. Gridded sources (GEBCO, ETOPO, ERA5, Hansen, GMW, GHSL, WorldPop, Black Marble) run from gigabytes to hundreds of gigabytes raw. They should be baked at build time into 2048×1024 (or case-study crop) JPEG/PNG textures, which matches the History chapter's 174–366 KB relief images. The repo's existing topojson toolchain covers vectors; mapshaper (MPL 2.0) and GDAL would be build-time additions only.

### Inferences
These are estimates for planning, not measured:
- **Earthquakes.** M≥6 events for 1900–2026 likely number in the low tens of thousands. Stored as typed columns quantised to about 0.01° plus depth, magnitude and a day number (roughly 10–12 bytes each), they should land around 150–300 KB before gzip. An M≥7 subset would be about a tenth of that. Pre-1960 completeness is weak, which the date ribbon should signal.
- **Volcanoes.** The GVP Holocene list (on the order of 1,000+ volcanoes) as name, lat, lon, type and last-eruption year would be about 30–60 KB.
- **Plates.** PB2002 boundaries with plate-boundary types, simplified with topojson-simplify to globe-scale detail, would likely be 50–150 KB.
- **Storm tracks.** A full IBTrACS track set is too large. Options: (a) one basin, or Category 3+ only; (b) 6-hourly points quantised to 0.1° with intensity class; (c) named case-study storms only. Each could fit in 100–400 KB.
- **Rivers.** Natural Earth 10m rivers filtered by scale rank should simplify to roughly 100–300 KB. HydroRIVERS should be used only for one case-study basin with ORD/upland-area thresholds, baked into a texture.
- **Rasters.** Following the History chapter's 2048 world texture plus sharp detail texture approach, each global layer (GEBCO shaded bathymetry, ERA5 or GISTEMP anomaly, Black Marble, GHSL or WorldPop density, Hansen loss for a window, GMW or Allen extent masks) should cost about 150–400 KB as JPEG, or less as palette PNG. Masks such as reefs and mangroves could be single-channel PNG or packed into one RGBA atlas.
- **Series.** Monthly Mauna Loa CO2 since 1958 (about 800 values), annual GISTEMP since 1880, monthly sea ice since 1978, and WPP/WDI/HDI values for case-study countries are each under 20 KB as JSON.
- **GDAL.** `gdalwarp`/`gdal_translate` are the usual tools for clipping and resampling GeoTIFF/netCDF to equirectangular images. Their licence (MIT/X-style) was not verified in this session. Like mapshaper, they are build-time tools, so their licences do not reach the shipped site.
- **Raw inputs.** Keep them in `tools/.cache/` (gitignored), exactly as for History, so only derived outputs enter the public repo.

## Q4. Are the download hosts slow or blocked from mainland China? (build time only)

### Takeaway
Evidence is patchy and dated. GreatFire measurements show some NASA and NOAA hosts blocked or partly disrupted: www.ngdc.noaa.gov 100% blocked (2026-06-24), earthobservatory.nasa.gov interfered with since 2025-12-29, climate.nasa.gov partly disrupted. github.com was 50% disrupted (2026-08-25), and a github.io page was partly disrupted. Meridian already found ETH Zürich downloads at about 20 KB/s. The practical rule: fetch raw inputs once (over a VPN, from outside China, or from mirrors such as AWS Open Data and Zenodo), keep them in `tools/.cache/`, and rebuild offline. ## Q5. Imagery, photographs and topographic sources usable as exam-style "resources"

### Takeaway
NASA (Earth Observatory, Visible Earth, Black/Blue Marble), USGS Landsat and Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery can be bundled freely with credit. Landsat and NASA are essentially public domain. Sentinel needs "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [Year]". Copernicus DEM GLO-30/90 is free with a fixed notice. Wikimedia Commons photos must be licensed file by file: credit title, author, source and licence, and keep CC BY-SA photos as separate works. Use OpenStreetMap only as baked map images ("produced works", attribution only), not as shipped vector extracts, which would be ODbL derivative databases. UK OS OpenData (OGL v3) and USGS topo data (public domain) are open national-survey sources for map-skills practice.

### Cited Findings
- Landsat: "there are no restrictions on Landsat data downloaded from the USGS; it can be used or redistributed as desired". USGS asks for a statement of the data source when citing, copying or reprinting (FAQ updated July 2025). — [USGS Landsat FAQ](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/are-there-any-restrictions-use-or-redistribution-landsat-data)
- An older USGS policy asks image captions to credit "USGS/NASA Landsat Program". Derived products "are not to be licensed or placed under copyright unless the original Landsat data are digitally merged with other data or are otherwise altered significantly". — [Landsat data distribution policy (NOAA InPort)](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/59574/printable-form)
- Copernicus Sentinel data: free, full and open access under Regulation (EU) No 377/2014 and Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1159/2013. Reproduction, distribution, adaptation and combination are permitted. Modified data must carry "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [Year]". Older text required "Copernicus Sentinel data [Year]" for unmodified data. — [ESA Sentinel Data Legal Notice](https://sentinel.esa.int/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice); [Copernicus Sentinel data licence (rev. 1)](https://ecds.ecmwf.int/licences/ec-sentinel)
- Copernicus DEM GLO-30 and GLO-90: free licence worldwide; the general public may download both. Unmodified data shared publicly needs "© DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved". Modified data needs "produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. …" plus a liability disclaimer. — [Copernicus Data Space: Copernicus DEM](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/node/1306); [GEE Copernicus DEM GLO-30](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_DEM_GLO30_2024_1)
- NASA Earth Observatory images are re-usable except where copyright is indicated; the mandatory credit is "NASA". Where a third-party copyright is marked, the holder's permission is needed. NASA hosts some images from non-US space agencies that are not necessarily public domain. — [NASA EO image use policy](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/ImageUse); [Visible Earth policy](https://www.visibleearth.nasa.gov/image-use-policy)
- Do not show NASA logos (insignia, logotype, seal), which are excluded from the general permission. — [NASA images and media](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
- Wikimedia Commons: "nearly all" files are under a free licence (usually CC BY, CC BY-SA or GFDL) or public domain. "Each media file has its licensing specified on its file description page". Hotlinking is "possible, but is not recommended". A reuse tool generates attribution lines. — [Commons: Reusing content outside Wikimedia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia)
- OSM attribution is needed only when a Produced Work is used publicly. Static images with fewer than 100 features or covering under 10,000 m² need no attribution. Attribution should link openstreetmap.org/copyright, and one credit per page with several images is enough. "© OpenStreetMap" alone does not meet the letter of the requirement (forum view). — [OSMF Attribution Guideline](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Attribution); [OSM community forum](https://community.openstreetmap.org/t/openstreetmap-attribution-in-small-static-image/5866)
- "The ODbL does not require any particular license for maps produced from ODbL data" (Wikipedia template; secondary). — [Template: ODbL OpenStreetMap](https://c.enwp.org/wiki/Template:ODbL_OpenStreetMap)
- UK Ordnance Survey OpenData: the standard attribution is "Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right [year]". OS moved from the OS OpenData Licence to the Open Government Licence in February 2015. The same attribution must be carried into any sub-licence. — [Marine Scotland OS OpenData](https://marine.gov.scot/copyright/os-opendata); [OSM wiki: OS OpenData Licence](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Ordnance_Survey_OpenData_Licence)
- OS Open Zoomstack via Digimap lists "OS Digimap Licence" terms, which differ from OGL. The terms depend on where the copy is obtained. — [EDINA Digimap OS Open Zoomstack](https://maps3.digimap.edina.ac.uk/webhelp/os/data_information/os_products/os_open_zoomstack.htm)
- USGS National Map data (US topographic base data) are free and public domain, with an acknowledgment requested. — [USGS National Map FAQ](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-are-terms-uselicensing-map-services-and-data-national-map)
- The Allen Coral Atlas satellite reef mosaic (Planet) is non-commercial ShareAlike. — [Allen Coral Atlas FAQ](https://allencoralatlas.org/faq/)

### Inferences
- **Case-study "resource" figures.** Make Landsat 8/9 or Sentinel-2 true- or false-colour crops at build time; for example, a river delta, a coastal spit, a rainforest deforestation front, or a city's growth from Landsat 1985 to 2025. Export as JPEG of about 100–300 KB each with the credit line printed in the figure caption. This mirrors exam inserts (photo + caption + source).
- **OS 1:50,000-style topographic map skills.** Bake OS OpenData (OGL) rasters, for example OS Open Zoomstack or OS VectorMap rendered by the build, for a UK practice extract. For a non-UK extract, render OSM into a static image with "© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, openstreetmap.org/copyright". Shipping OSM GeoJSON would make that file an ODbL derivative database, which must then be offered under ODbL. That is legal, but it must stay separate from the CC BY-NC-SA files.
- **Wikimedia photos.** For each photo, keep a sidecar record (title, author, source URL, licence, changes) and print it under the image. Avoid "NC" or "ND" files only if the site may one day go commercial; ND files cannot be cropped or annotated. Exam-style annotation counts as an adaptation, so ND photos are unusable for annotated resources.
- **Avoid as resources.** Google Earth/Maps, Esri basemap tiles, Planet or Maxar commercial imagery, and news-agency photos (AP, Reuters, Getty) are proprietary. Their terms were not researched here; this is general knowledge, so treat it as "check first".

### Gaps
- The current EROS "Data Citation" wording for Landsat was not retrieved.
- OS Terrain 50 and OS Open Zoomstack licence specifics on the current OS Data Hub were not confirmed.
- Whether earthobservatory.nasa.gov remains a separately maintained site in 2026 was not verified (GreatFire still tests it).
- No source settles whether a heavily styled static map of OSM data can ever trigger share-alike. The OSMF view is that it is a Produced Work requiring attribution only.

## Q6. Which well-known datasets must not be used, or only with care?

### Takeaway
Do not vendor UNEP-WCMC data (warm-water coral reefs and other General Data License layers, plus the WDPA). Its licence bans redistribution "in whole or in part by any means", including web downloads and interactive web maps, and bans sub-licensing within derived works. Use only with care: Smithsonian GVP (non-commercial, educational terms that are not a public licence), HydroSHEDS core products (custom agreement, no stand-alone redistribution, WWF credit), Berkeley Earth (BY-NC, conflicting BY-NC-SA listing), Quantarctica (mixed per-layer licences), ODbL sources (OSM, WorldPop OSM-derived, Allen Coral Atlas OSM layers), CC BY-SA and ND photos, World Bank "Restricted Data" indicators, and OWID's third-party series. Watch for recently changed terms: the Copernicus licence (CC BY from 2 July 2025), GVP versions (5.3.4 → 5.4.0 during 2026), Black Marble v1 (ended January 2025), NSIDC Sea Ice Index v4 (Basic service) and GEBCO_2026.

### Cited Findings
- UNEP-WCMC General Data License (excluding WDPA): "Neither the Data nor any work derived from it may be put to Commercial Use without the prior written permission of the Director of UNEP-WCMC". "Commercial Use" includes "any use by an individual or entity operating within or on behalf of an entity other than a not-for-profit organisation". "The Data may not be sub-licensed in whole or in part including within Derivative Works without the prior written permission". "You may not redistribute the Data in whole or in part by any means including electronic formats such as web downloads, through web services, through interactive web maps that grant users download access, KML Files or through file transfer protocols." — [UNEP-WCMC General Data License](https://www.unep-wcmc.org/policies/general-data-license-excluding-wdpa)
- The coral reef layer is listed as "Not for commercial use" with restricted access (requests to the point of contact), and as restricted by the UNEP-WCMC General Data License. — [SPREP mirror](https://rmi-data.sprep.org/dataset/319); [Open Development Cambodia](https://data.opendevelopmentcambodia.net/km/dataset/global-distribution-of-coral-reefs)
- Protected Planet / WDPA data are governed by a separate WDPA Data License. A newer UNEP-WCMC resources page describes non-commercial versus commercial licences where "redistribution is either restricted or requires 'sharing alike'". — [UNEP-WCMC policies](https://www.unep-wcmc.org/en/policies); [UNEP-WCMC Resources product page](https://resources.unep-wcmc.org/products/e56277812f854e7ca8fa9282ee323c5e)
- GMW vectors are also distributed through UNEP-WCMC. The CC BY 4.0 v3.0 files are on Zenodo, so the Zenodo copy is the one to use. — [CEOS GMW](https://ceos.org/gst/mangrove.html); [Zenodo 6894273](https://zenodo.org/record/6894273)
- Smithsonian non-CC0 content: personal, educational, non-commercial uses only, consistent with fair use. A commentator calls the wording legally confusing. — [Smithsonian Terms of Use](https://www.si.edu/termsofuse); [Michael Weinberg on Smithsonian Open Access](https://michaelweinberg.org/blog/2020/03/01/smithsonian-open-access/)
- HydroSHEDS: no stand-alone distribution (v1.3), WWF ownership of modifications flagged in the v1.4 reading, and HydroLAKES asks not to redistribute the full dataset in original format without permission. — [HydroSHEDS TechDoc v1.3 legacy](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/602eb43d42a3b5553a4f134c/624361e8ace0af1ff6fa65fa_HydroSHEDS_TechDoc_v1_3_legacy.pdf); [neer-vazhvu PR #463](https://github.com/SundareshPrasanna/neer-vazhvu/pull/463); [HydroLAKES TechDoc](https://data.hydrosheds.org/file/technical-documentation/HydroLAKES_TechDoc_v10.pdf)
- Berkeley Earth is CC BY-NC 4.0, but the Copernicus document lists BY-NC-SA 4.0. — [Berkeley Earth data](https://berkeleyearth.org/data/); [Copernicus in-situ licence](https://ads.atmosphere.copernicus.eu/licences/insitu-gridded-observations-global-and-regional)
- Quantarctica layers have individual licences. A third-party article calls the package "free for noncommercial use", which contradicts the CC BY 4.0 package licence. — [Quantarctica ReadMe](https://data.pgc.umn.edu/gis/packages/quantarctica/Quantarctica3/Quantarctica_ReadMe.txt); [Geography Realm](https://www.geographyrealm.com/quantarctica-free-gis-data-qgis/)
- WorldPop OSM- and Microsoft-derived datasets are ODbL with share-alike. — [WorldPop hub](https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/summary?id=24767)
- World Bank Restricted Data cannot be redistributed without contacting the original provider. — [World Bank Microdata terms](https://microdata.worldbank.org/terms-of-use)
- OWID third-party data keeps the original authors' licences. — [OWID co2-data README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/owid/co2-data/master/README.md)
- The Allen Coral Atlas site includes OSM and Protected Planet data that need their own attribution. — [Allen Coral Atlas FAQ](https://allencoralatlas.org/faq/)
- BY-NC-SA and BY-SA cannot be combined into one adaptation. — [Wikimedia Meta guide](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Open_Content_-_A_Practical_Guide_to_Using_Creative_Commons_Licences/The_Creative_Commons_licencing_scheme)
- ND works cannot be used in publicly shared adaptations. — [University of Guelph guide](https://books.lib.uoguelph.ca/copyrightcc/?p=46)
- Recent changes:
  - Copernicus CC BY from 2 July 2025. — [ECMWF forum](https://forum.ecmwf.int/t/cc-by-licence-to-replace-licence-to-use-copernicus-products-on-02-july-2025/13464)
  - Black Marble v1 production stopped 31 Jan 2025. — [LAADS alert](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/alerts-and-issues/171686)
  - NSIDC Sea Ice Index v4 moved to Basic service. — [NSIDC G02135](https://nsidc.org/data/g02135)
  - GVP VOTW 5.4.0 (Aug 2026). — [GVP VOTW](https://volcano.si.edu/gvp_votw.cfm)
  - GEBCO_2026 is listed. — [GEBCO table](https://www.gebco.net/data_and_products/gridded_bathymetry_data/)
  - The GHSL portal domain is now human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu. — [GHSL how to cite](https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/GHSLhowToCite.php)
- WorldPop country files adjusted to UN totals use WPP 2019 as the reference, not WPP 2024. Mixing them with WPP 2024 figures on the same page will show inconsistencies. — [WorldPop hub](https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/summary?id=24767)

### Inferences
- **Coral reefs.** Use the Allen Coral Atlas (CC BY 4.0) reef extent or geomorphic zones, or Natural Earth reefs (public domain), instead of the UNEP-WCMC layer. A static, non-downloadable texture probably still counts as "redistributing the Data in part" and a "Derivative Work", and the site's JSON/PNG files are downloadable by anyone. So even a baked UNEP-WCMC texture is unsafe without written permission.
- **Volcanoes.** If the GVP terms seem too uncertain for a public repo, a lower-risk route is to ship only volcano names and positions with a clear GVP credit; facts such as names and coordinates are weakly protected. Alternatively, ask GVP for permission, or check NOAA NCEI's public-domain volcano database (not researched).
- **Rivers.** Use Natural Earth (public domain) for the globe. Use HydroRIVERS only as a baked, case-study-scale image, credited "incorporates data from HydroSHEDS © WWF", never as a downloadable vector file. Ideally email the HydroSHEDS team, because their licence reserves stand-alone redistribution.
- **Temperature.** Use GISTEMP or ERA5 for "global temperature" graphics. Berkeley Earth is legal on this non-commercial site but adds an NC tag that would prevent anyone reusing the layer commercially. It also needs its own licence notice because of the BY-NC vs BY-NC-SA ambiguity.
- **OSM.** Never mix OSM-derived vectors into `borders.json` or any CC BY-NC-SA file: the ODbL and CC NC-SA share-alike terms conflict.
- **Dated data.** Pin exact versions and download dates in the licence file (as History does for CShapes). Several sources update constantly: IBTrACS three times a week, ComCat continuously, GVP versions during 2026.

### Gaps
- No source was found on whether UNEP-WCMC grants permission to educational non-profit sites; contacting them was out of scope.
- The NOAA NCEI Significant Volcanic Eruptions and Significant Earthquakes databases (likely public domain, with impact and death data useful for "hazard impact" scenes) were not researched.
- The licence of Natural Earth's own reefs layer beyond the global public-domain statement was not separately verified, and its detail is likely coarse.
