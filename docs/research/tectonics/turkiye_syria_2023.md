## 1. Causes and tectonic setting (scene 19; feeds scene 8)

### Takeaway
The earthquakes happened on the East Anatolian Fault Zone (EAFZ). This is a left-lateral strike-slip (conservative) boundary along which the Anatolian plate moves west, squeezed out by the Arabian plate pushing north. The fault ends at a triple junction with the Dead Sea Fault (the Arabia–Africa boundary) near Kahramanmaraş. Before 2023, its south-western segments (Pazarcık, Erkenek) had been quiet for about 500 years, since the AD 1513 earthquake. Geodetic studies say enough strain had built up there for an earthquake of about Mw 7.5.

### Cited Findings
- USGS: the M7.8 "resulted from strike-slip faulting at shallow depth" within the broad East Anatolian fault zone. — [USGS event page us6000jllz, "M 7.8 – Pazarcik earthquake, Kahramanmaras earthquake sequence"](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000jllz/) (snippet; earlier repo note [syllabus_and_topic_fit.md](../../docs/research/geography/syllabus_and_topic_fit.md) line 199 quotes the same wording)
- The sequence began with a Mw ≈7.0 sub-event at Narlı on a subsidiary splay fault. It was "instantly followed by the Mw 7.8 Pazarcık Earthquake bilaterally rupturing multiple structural elements in a complex triple junction system". The Mw 7.6 Elbistan event came about nine hours later. — [ITU, "February 2023 Kahramanmaraş Earthquake Sequence: 'supercycle' events within a complex tectonic setting"](https://polen.itu.edu.tr/entities/publication/d0ffa68b-17f7-40dd-aeec-e221588d67aa/full)
- The sequence occurred "in a critical tectonic setting connecting the Dead Sea Fault and the Adana-Cilicia-Hatay basin"; the Pazarcık rupture "reached the Dead Sea Fault". "The tectonic extrusion of Anatolia is accommodated by the North Anatolian and East Anatolian strike-slip faults." A Turkish study's figure names the "Maraş Triple Junction" and the Dead Sea Fault Zone. — [EGU24 session abstracts, Copernicus Meetings](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU24/session/48675); [EGU24-17052](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU24/EGU24-17052.html); [figure in Sci. ### Inferences
- Scene 19 body (suggested): "Arabia pushes north into Eurasia. Anatolia, the block Türkiye stands on, is squeezed west along two great sideways faults. The southern one is the East Anatolian Fault. Near Kahramanmaraş it meets the Dead Sea Fault, the edge between Arabia and Africa: three plates at one point." This is a sideways (conservative) boundary, so the chapter should say there are no volcanoes on it. That links to scene 8's mistake card ("conservative boundaries have volcanoes").

## 2. The event: two mainshocks, rupture, ground displacement, shaking and aftershocks (scenes 20–21, 11)

### Takeaway
USGS: Mw 7.8 at 01:17:34 UTC (04:17 local) on 6 February 2023, epicentre 37.226°N 37.014°E, 10 km deep, strike-slip. Then Mw 7.5 at 10:24:48 UTC (13:24 local), 38.011°N 37.196°E, 7.4 km deep, about 95 km away. AFAD gives Mw 7.7 and Mw 7.6. The M7.8 ruptured about 300 km of fault (310 km in satellite mapping) and the M7.5 about 140 km. Sideways ground offsets reached about 7–9 m. USGS ShakeMap peaked at MMI X. AFAD counted about 38,000 aftershocks by May 2023.

### Inferences
- **Which magnitudes to use:** USGS 7.8 and 7.5. The USGS ShakeMap, PAGER and ComCat data the globe will bake all use them, and they are the international reference. Add one line: "Türkiye's AFAD measured 7.7 and 7.6". Students will meet both numbers in Turkish sources and in the CUP coursebook.
- **Which depth to use:** "about 10 km" (USGS) for the M7.8. Call both events shallow-focus.
- **Which rupture length to use:** "about 300 km" for the M7.8 (310 km satellite, 270 ± 10 km field, >300 km SSA) and "about 140 km" for the M7.5.
- **Which offset to use:** "up to about 7–8 m sideways"; the peer-reviewed range is 7.3 m (field) to 7.5 ± 0.8 m (satellite) for the M7.8. Do not use the 4 m InSAR figure as the fault offset. It is line-of-sight motion, as the ESA caption says.
- Scene 21: the ESA Sentinel-2 "3 m east–west / 5 m north–south" map is the best figure for students. Before showing it, check the licence for redistribution: ESA images are usually CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO, but that was not verified.
- Mistake card for scene 20: "It was one earthquake." In fact there were two mainshocks (M7.8 and M7.5) about 9 hours apart, plus an M6.7 eleven minutes after the first and an M6.3 two weeks later.
- [derived] The M7.8 released about 10^(1.5×0.3) ≈ 2.8 times the energy of the M7.5, and about 178 times the energy of the M6.3 aftershock (10^(1.5×1.5)). This uses the standard energy–magnitude relation. Check the wording against the USGS magnitude page before publishing.

## 3. Impacts in Türkiye (scene 22)

### Takeaway
The official Turkish toll is **53,537 dead and 107,213 injured**, given by Interior Minister Ali Yerlikaya at AFAD in early February 2024. Eleven provinces with more than 14 million people were affected. About 230,000 buildings collapsed or had to be demolished. About 3 million people were displaced. Hatay (Antakya) was the worst-hit province.

## 4. Impacts in Syria and why northwest Syria's impacts were worse (scene 22)

### Takeaway
Syria had about **5,900–6,000 deaths**. The most-used figure is AFP's 5,951 (1,414 in government-held areas and 4,537 in opposition-held northwest Syria). About 8.8 million people were in the hardest-hit areas. Northwest Syria fared worst for three reasons: 12 years of war had already left 4.1 million people in need of aid; Bab al-Hawa was the only crossing the UN Security Council allowed; and the first UN convoy arrived only on day four.

## 5. Economic damage: World Bank GRADE vs the Presidency of Strategy and Budget (TERRA)

### Takeaway
These two figures are not contradictory; they measure different things. The World Bank's rapid GRADE estimate (report dated 20 Feb, press release 27 Feb 2023) is **US$34.2 bn of direct physical damage**, about 4% of Türkiye's 2021 GDP. The government's TERRA assessment (17 March 2023) is **US$103.6 bn (TRY 2 trillion)** of damage plus losses, about 9% of forecast 2023 GDP. Syria's GRADE figure is **US$5.1 bn**.

### Cited Findings
- **World Bank GRADE, Türkiye:**
  - US$34.2 bn of direct physical damage, about 4% of 2021 GDP;
  - residential about $18 bn, non-residential $9.7 bn, infrastructure about $6.4 bn;
  - recovery and reconstruction costs "could be roughly double";
  - it excludes indirect and secondary impacts, was made remotely in two weeks, and "may increase further" (citing the M6.3 of 20 Feb).
  - The report is dated 20 Feb 2023 and the press release 27 Feb 2023.
  - — [World Bank press release, 27 Feb 2023, "Earthquake damage in Türkiye estimated to exceed $34 billion"](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2023/02/27/earthquake-damage-in-turkiye-estimated-to-exceed-34-billion-world-bank-disaster-assessment-report); [GRADE report PDF](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022723021250141/pdf/P1788430aeb62f08009b2302bd4074030fb.pdf); [GFDRR](https://www.gfdrr.org/en/node/11183)
- **TERRA:**
  - The Presidency of Strategy and Budget (SBO) published "Türkiye Earthquakes Recovery and Reconstruction Assessment" on 17 March 2023;
  - it gives US$103.6 bn (TRY 2 trillion), equal to 9% of forecast 2023 GDP;
  - it uses a modified PDNA method, with support from the UN, EU and World Bank, and was prepared ahead of the Brussels donors' conference of 20 March 2023;
  - the UN describes it as total damage and losses.
  - — [Ministry of Treasury and Finance, "SBO released 'Türkiye Earthquakes Recovery and Reconstruction Assessment' Report"](https://en.hmb.gov.tr/duyuru/sbo-published-turkiye-earthquakes-recovery-and-reconstruction-assessment); [ReliefWeb copy](https://reliefweb.int/report/turkiye/turkiye-earthquakes-recovery-and-reconstruction-assessment); [UN Türkiye/UNDP](https://turkiye.un.org/en/225357-mapping-path-recovery-undp-urges-solidarity-earthquake-hit-t%C3%BCrkiye)
- Türkiye and its partners estimated recovery and reconstruction costs at more than $81 bn. — [World Bank Türkiye Earthquake Recovery and Reconstruction Project (P180849)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099011825052035952/pdf/P18084917ea92a0f21842b1f8041a381aef.pdf)
- Erdoğan, at the Brussels conference: about $104 bn (€96 bn). — [Al-Monitor/AFP](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2023/03/donors-vow-funds-turkey-quake-reconstruction-cost-top-100bn) [secondary]
- A rupture study gives "~$119B in damage". — [NSF PAR record](https://par.nsf.gov/biblio/10585045) (via repo note)
- A 2026 SBO report says about $91.5 bn had been spent by the end of 2025 on compensation and disaster-risk reduction, and calls the disaster the largest economic loss in modern Turkish history. — [Türkiye Today](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/nation/2023-earthquake-update-turkiye-confronts-reconstruction-progress-and-accountability-q-3214082) or [Rapor Bülteni on the SBB 2026 report](https://raporbulteni.substack.com/p/47-sbb-2023-kahramanmaras-ve-hatay) [attribution unclear; secondary]
- **Syria GRADE:** $5.1 bn (see section 4).

### Inferences
- Teach the difference as "damage vs damage + losses". GRADE counts only buildings and infrastructure physically broken. TERRA adds lost output, jobs and economic disruption. Recommended wording: "The World Bank estimated US$34.2 billion of direct damage (4% of GDP). Türkiye's own assessment put the total cost at US$103.6 billion, about 9% of GDP." Drop the plan's "about $119 bn". It comes from one paper and is not an official assessment.
- Scene 22 scale device: $103.6 bn ≈ 9% of a year's national output.

### Gaps

- The World Bank GRADE report's per-province split for Türkiye was not seen.

## 6. Responses: immediate (scenes 23; 4.4.3 "responses")

### Takeaway
AFAD led the national response, with a three-month state of emergency over 10 provinces from 7 February. About **255 international USAR teams** came (199 at peak, 49 of them INSARAG-classified), with about **11,320 personnel and 306 dogs**. The EU activated its Civil Protection Mechanism on day one. UN flash appeals asked **US$1 bn for Türkiye (16 Feb)** and **US$397.6 m for Syria (14 Feb)**. A Brussels donors' conference on **20 March** raised **€7 bn**. China sent an 82-member rescue team to Antakya.

### Cited Findings
- **State of emergency**:
  - Erdoğan declared it on 7 Feb 2023 under Article 119 of the Constitution, for three months, in 10 provinces: Kahramanmaraş, Hatay, Gaziantep, Osmaniye, Malatya, Adıyaman, Adana, Diyarbakır, Kilis and Şanlıurfa;

## 8. Management: building code, the 2018 amnesty, prosecutions, early warning (Evaluate block, scene 23 and scene 29)

### Takeaway
Türkiye had a modern building code: TBDY-2018, in force from 1 January 2019 and following big revisions after the 1999 Kocaeli earthquake. But the 2018 zoning amnesty (imar barışı) gave legal papers to an estimated **294,165** unapproved buildings in the quake-hit provinces. A lower estimate is 70,000–75,000. By July 2023 prosecutors had **arrested 351 people, including 118 contractors**. Shaking in some places exceeded what the design models predicted. So the evidence points to enforcement and old building stock, not the absence of a code, as the main cause of deaths.

### Cited Findings
- **TBDY-2018** (Türkiye Bina Deprem Yönetmeliği, Turkish Building Earthquake Code) entered into force on 1 January 2019, replacing the 2007 regulation (DBYBHY-2007), which was judged insufficient for design and performance. — [Süleyman Demirel Univ. journal (doi:10.19113/sdufenbed.1150536)](https://dx.doi.org/10.19113/sdufenbed.1150536)
- EERI's Buildings Reconnaissance Team covered more than 160 buildings at about 130 sites, mostly reinforced concrete. Their construction dates ranged "from pre-2000s to as new as a few years old". Turkish codes "underwent significant changes after the 1999 Kocaeli earthquake and most recently in 2018". — [Earthquake Spectra (SAGE) 2024, EERI reconnaissance article](https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.1177/87552930241307884) (abstract only; paywalled)
- The code's elastic design spectra were "insufficient to represent earthquake behaviour" for the 2023 events. — [Buildings (MDPI), April 2024, via DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/574bc767ce084657a0ad0faaa3e17ff5)
- PGA in Şanlıurfa, Hatay, Kahramanmaraş and Elazığ was higher than ground-motion models predicted. — [ITU/Kocaeli strong-motion paper](https://research.itu.edu.tr/en/publications/characteristics-of-strong-ground-motion-from-the-2023-mw-78-and-m-2/)

### Inferences

- **Strategy: building codes (protection).**

- **Strategy: prediction and early warning (monitoring/prediction).**

  - *Verdict:* forecasting tells you where to enforce codes; it cannot replace them.
- **Strategy: planning and response (international aid, rapid assessment).**
  - *For:* 255 USAR teams and about 300 live rescues by INSARAG-classified teams; damage estimates in 2 weeks (GRADE) and 6 weeks (TERRA) unlocked €7 bn of pledges.
  - *Against:* in NW Syria a single crossing delayed aid until day four.

- The site already defines focus and epicentre (in its own words, without a source): "Focus: the point underground where an earthquake starts. Epicentre: the point on the Earth's surface directly above the focus." — `/home/user/meridian/assets/js/data/practice-geography.js` lines 137–138; `/home/user/meridian/assets/js/data/geography.js` line 275
- The site quiz already says "The moment magnitude scale (like the Richter scale) measures energy released. The Mercalli scale measures the effects". — `/home/user/meridian/assets/js/data/geography.js` line 300

### Inferences
- Scene 10 mistake card: "The epicentre is where the earthquake starts." In fact it starts at the focus (hypocentre), about 10 km below Pazarcık district for the M7.8 (USGS). The epicentre is the point on the surface directly above it.
- Scene 11 mistake card: "A magnitude 7.8 is only a little bigger than 7.5." In fact, each whole step on the magnitude scale is about 10 times the ground-shaking amplitude and about 32 times the energy. So 7.8 vs 7.5 is about 2.8 times the energy [derived; verify the "×32" wording on the USGS page listed under Gaps].
- Scene 11 can show the USGS ShakeMap as the "Mercalli" picture (MMI X near the fault, falling with distance) next to one "Mw 7.8" label. This shows "magnitude = size of the source, intensity = shaking at a place".
- The site quiz's phrase "like the Richter scale" is acceptable at IGCSE level. But the chapter should say that Richter (local magnitude, ML) is an older scale for nearby, smaller quakes, and that USGS uses moment magnitude for large ones. This needs the USGS source below before publishing.

### Gaps
These are proposed facts and USGS pages to verify; I did not open or search them this session. Treat each as unverified until checked.
- Hypocentre is "the point within the earth where an earthquake rupture starts"; epicentre is "the point on the earth's surface vertically above the hypocenter". — candidate: USGS Earthquake Hazards Program glossary, https://earthquake.usgs.gov/learn/glossary/
