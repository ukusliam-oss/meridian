## Q1. Plate boundaries by type (PB2002 via fraxen, PB2002 steps, Hasterok et al. 2022)

### Takeaway
fraxen's PB2002 `boundaries` layer does **not** keep Bird's boundary types beyond one flag: `Type` is `"subduction"` on 65 of 241 lines and empty on the rest. The types and velocities are in the PB2002 **steps** file, which assigns one of 7 classes to each of 5,819 steps (558 KB CSV). Hasterok et al. 2022 has the most IGCSE-friendly types (spreading centre, subduction zone, collision zone, dextral/sinistral transform, extension zone), but its GitHub copy is licensed **GPL-3.0**.

### Inferences
- Building PB2002 boundary types means reading `PB2002_steps.csv` (558 KB) and drawing each step as a coloured segment, or assigning each boundary the dominant class of its steps. The same file gives velocities (mm/a) and convergence direction for the chevrons. Mapping to IGCSE vocabulary: OSR → constructive/divergent; SUB and OCB → destructive; CCB → collision; OTF and CTF → conservative/transform; CRB → continental rift. The boundaries file alone gives only subduction versus everything else.
- Hasterok matches the syllabus better (e.g. the EAF as a transform, which suits the DSE "conservative" story), but GPL-3.0 is a software copyleft licence. A derived file would have to be released as GPL-3.0, with the source form available, kept as its own file next to the CC BY-NC-SA borders file. PB2002 under ODC-By has no such condition. Recommendation: PB2002 steps for the globe; use Hasterok only if its Zenodo record turns out to carry a more permissive data licence.

### Inferences
- For a dark, ocean-first globe, the cleanest public-domain stack: `GRAY_50M_SR_OB` (land relief plus CleanTOPO2 ocean floor) or `OB_50M` for the ocean colour, recoloured at build time and downsampled to the engine's 2048-wide world texture. Regional detail crops can come from Terrain Tiles z5–z8 (ETOPO1 and GMTED/SRTM, all US public domain outside Canada and Europe) or from Copernicus GLO-90.
- Terrain Tiles are Web Mercator and stop at about ±85.05°, so they need reprojecting to equirectangular and a polar fill (e.g. from basemap `etopo1.jpg` or Natural Earth). basemap's `etopo1.jpg` is a pre-coloured ETOPO1 image, usable directly at 5400×2700 but with fixed colours.
- Prefer basemap-data `bmng.jpg` (full colour) over the GMT caches (256-colour palette, banding) and the three-globe images (provenance unstated). The freetiler tiles are a GIBS mosaic in Web Mercator that would need stitching and reprojection.
- From mainland China, GMT's `china.generic-mapping-tools.org` mirror would be the natural place to fetch the GEBCO 2023 and SRTM15+ grids at 6′–10′ (3–8 MB each).

### Gaps
- The Copernicus DEM licence text and the SRTM15+ licence were not read.
- The original sources of the three basemap-data images are not named in the wheel METADATA. NASA BMNG, NOAA ETOPO1 and shadedrelief.com are inferences from the file names, and the MIT label on them is the packager's.
- Whether `china.generic-mapping-tools.org` is online today, and reachable from the school, is untested.
